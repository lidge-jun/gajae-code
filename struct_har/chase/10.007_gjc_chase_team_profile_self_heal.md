## 왜 gap인가

Upstream GJC dev now distinguishes a genuinely GJC-launched tmux leader whose session lost the `@gjc-profile` option from a foreign/unmanaged tmux session. A missing tag is self-healed only when the pane carries the launch marker; otherwise the guard still rejects the session. JWC must preserve the same ownership safety if/when the `team` workflow surface is reconciled.
